Last updated: October 2026

30-day returns
Unused items in their original, unopened packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Email elevatepoolandspacare@gmail.com with your order number to start a return. Please wait for our return instructions before sending anything back.

Items we can't accept back

• Opened or used chemicals, test strips and reagents. For safety reasons, these can't be resold.
• Electrical parts that have been installed or wired in.
• Special-order or custom items, unless they arrive damaged or wrong.

Damaged, defective or wrong items
If something arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, email us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping, at no cost to you.

Refunds
Once we receive and check your return, we refund the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the return is due to our error. For change-of-mind returns, return shipping is paid by the customer.

Cancellations
You can cancel any order, including pre-orders, free of charge before it ships. Your card is not charged until shipment.

============================================================

Last updated: October 2026

Where we ship
We currently ship within the contiguous United States (lower 48 states). We do not ship to P.O. boxes, APO/FPO addresses, Alaska or Hawaii at this time.

Processing time
In-stock orders usually leave within 1–3 business days. Many items ship directly from our supplier partners, so one order may arrive in more than one package.

Delivery time
Most orders arrive 3–7 business days after shipping. You'll get a tracking number by email once your order ships.

Pre-orders
Pre-order items show an expected ship date on the product. Your card is not charged until the item ships. If the date slips by more than 30 days, we'll let you know and you can cancel for a full refund.

Pool & spa chemicals
Some chemicals are regulated as hazardous materials for shipping. These go by ground only, may have extra handling charges, and may not be available in every state. Any restrictions are shown at checkout.

Lost or damaged packages
If your tracking shows delivered but you can't find your package, or it arrives damaged, email elevatepoolandspacare@gmail.com within 7 days and we'll help sort it out with the carrier.
